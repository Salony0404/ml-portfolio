# Transformer-Based Text Summarization

## Overview
This project explores transformer-based models for abstractive text summarization. Multiple architectures, including **Pegasus** and **Funnel Transformer**, are evaluated to understand their performance on summarization tasks.

The work is implemented primarily through Jupyter notebooks and focuses on experimentation, evaluation, and comparison of different transformer models.

---

## Problem Statement
Automatic text summarization aims to condense large documents into concise summaries while preserving key information.  
The objective of this project is to study how different transformer architectures perform on summarization tasks and to analyze their strengths and limitations.

---

## Dataset
- Text datasets collected from publicly available sources
- Dataset links and references are maintained for reproducibility
- Data preprocessing includes cleaning, normalization, and formatting for transformer models

---

## Methodology
1. Data cleaning and preprocessing
2. Tokenization using transformer-specific tokenizers
3. Model training and fine-tuning
4. Inference and qualitative evaluation
5. Model comparison across architectures

---

## Models Explored
- **Pegasus Transformer**
- **Funnel Transformer**
- Baseline transformer configurations

---

## Experiments
- Training Pegasus for abstractive summarization
- Testing and inference on unseen samples
- Comparative experiments with Funnel Transformer
- Exploratory analysis of summarization quality
